**Phase:** Cross-cutting
**Guiding principle:** figures carry a date; if older than six months they get reconfirmed

> Prices verified in September 2026. Gateway percentages vary by negotiation and volume; request a direct quote before setting the sale price.

## Realistic floor

70 to 120 USD/month in infrastructure, plus 4% to 6% of every sale.

Initial development is separate and one time.

## Per sale, by route

| Route | Cost per sale | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| A · Local gateway | ~5.5% to 6% | Gateway + VAT + Shopify surcharge |
| B · US entity | ~3.5% to 4.5% | Shopify Payments without surcharge |

Break-even between routes: USD 5,000 to 6,000 in monthly sales. See [payments.md](payments.md).

## What can grow

- **Hosting and database** grow with traffic and orders, but the album (P3) is what can raise hosting the most because of asset weight.
- **Shopify apps** is the most elastic line. Each app is justified by a real need from a drop.
- **Transactional email** grows with orders. At 5 emails per order, 1,000 orders/month is 5,000 emails.

# Domain · Trademark

**Phase:** Before P1
**Guiding principle:** register it before the first drop
**Decision:** made. Registration in the US. See [decision 0006](../decisions/0006-trademark-registration-in-the-us.md)

> **Notice.** Not legal advice. Fees verified in September 2026: USPTO base of 350 per class since the January 2025 change; SIC per Resolution 6167 of 2026. Confirm at uspto.gov and sic.gov.co before paying.

## Why it is here

It is not a technical domain, but it blocks the web domain name, the social handles, and the name the store sells under. If the name changes after drop one, the landing, the store, the emails, and the printed codes all change.

## Name

**Art and Racing.** It is the name of the project, the brand, and this repository.

### What is left to research

The path is decided, not the detail. Before filing, close:

1. **Real total cost:** base fee per class, US attorney, surcharges for description, and the cost of the declaration of use if filing on intent to use.
2. **Operations:** who files, who is the owner, who receives USPTO correspondence, and who responds to an *office action* within the deadline.
3. **Owner:** if payments route B is taken, the LLC. If not, decide between a natural person or a Colombian entity, and how it is assigned later if the LLC is incorporated.
4. **Availability search** in TESS (USPTO) and by phonetic similarity, not only exact match.

## Colombia · SIC

Not the selected path, but kept as reference in case filing in Colombia is decided later.

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Fee | COP 1,347,500 per class |
| SME rate | COP 970,000 per class |
| Time | 6 to 8 months |
| Term | 10 years |
| Rule | First to file wins |

Risk to keep in mind: registering only in the US does not protect the name in Colombia. If someone files it first at the SIC, they can block its commercial use there. Accepting that risk or covering it later is a business decision.

1. Name availability search at USPTO (and at SIC as reference).
2. Verify the web domain is available with the same name.
3. Verify social handles with the same name.

All three must match. If one is unavailable, reconsider before paying fees.

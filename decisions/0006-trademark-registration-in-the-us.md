# 0006 · Trademark registration in the US

**Status:** `accepted`
**Owner:** business
**Deadline:** before drop one
**Blocks:** — (does not block code, but blocks the final name on landing, store, emails, and printed codes)

> Not legal advice.

## Context

The trademark has to be registered before the first drop. The two jurisdictions evaluated were Colombia (SIC) and the US (USPTO). Detail on fees, timelines, and classes in [domains/trademark.md](../domains/trademark.md).

## Decision

**Art and Racing** is registered in the US with the USPTO. That is the selected path.

Total costs and operations (US attorney for foreign applicants, filing basis, owner, maintenance) are still to be researched. That research does not reopen the decision; it executes it.

## Why

- The target audience and USD sales point to the US.
- If payments route B is taken ([decision 0001](0001-payment-gateway-and-jurisdiction.md)), the LLC can be the owner and everything stays under the same entity.

## Consequences

- The name of the project, the repository, and the brand is Art and Racing.
- Before filing: USPTO search, domain, and handles with the same name.
- Registering only in the US does not protect the name in Colombia. That risk is accepted for now and can be covered later by filing at the SIC.
- Decision 0001 inherits a constraint: if no LLC is incorporated, another owner has to be defined along with how it is assigned later.
- Pending, with owner and date to assign: real total cost, who files, who responds to *office actions*.
